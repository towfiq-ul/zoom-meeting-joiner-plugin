Privacy Policy

Meeting Invite Scanner does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything runs 100% locally on your machine.

Data Collection & Handling

  • No personal data is collected: There is no telemetry, no tracking, no analytics, no error reporting, and no backend server of any kind.
  • Images stay on your device: Screenshot or photo files loaded into the extension are processed entirely in memory via bundled client-side WebAssembly OCR. They are never uploaded or transmitted over any network, and are discarded when you close or reload the popup.
  • Pasted invite text: Parsed directly in your browser. Nothing is sent to any external service.
  • Meeting details cache: The extracted meeting ID and passcode can be cached locally for up to 8 hours using chrome.storage.local solely so you can rejoin without re-scanning. The cache is retained on your device only, and can be cleared at any time via the "Clear" button in the popup.

Permissions Used

Permission Purpose
storage Saves your preferred display name and holds the temporary 8-hour meeting cache locally on your device. Nothing in storage is ever synced or sent off your device.
wasm-unsafe-eval (CSP) Allows execution of the bundled Tesseract.js WebAssembly OCR engine locally inside the extension. It does not load, fetch, or execute any remote code.

Offline Operation & Security

The OCR engine (Tesseract.js), its WebAssembly binaries, and the English trained language model are all pre-packaged in the extension's local files. Content Security Policy restricts scripts to extension-packaged assets only. The extension functions fully without an active internet connection.
